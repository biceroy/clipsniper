It listens, reads the screen, and lets a fixed rule decide.

  1. Listen

    One ffmpeg pass pulls the audio and a frame every 30 seconds from Kick at 20× realtime. Loudness against a five-minute median marks the spikes.

  2. Read

    Claude gets three frames around each spike and reads the slot, bet, win, banner and bonus stage off the game UI.

  3. Decide

    100× the bet or $1,000 and up counts as a big win. The same frames get the same verdict on every run, and a long bonus collapses into one entry with its final total.

  4. Cut

    Exports copy the 1080p60 source with no re-encode. Vertical exports stack the cam over the game for TikTok and Shorts, captions optional.

Limits and privacy

  • Frames leave your machine for Anthropic, under your key. Audio goes to Groq or OpenAI only if you add a transcription key.
  • Quiet wins can slip by. A win outside the top 60 spikes gets missed. Raise the count in Settings for a calm streamer.
  • The app binds to 127.0.0.1. No telemetry, no account.
  • Kick only, last 30 VODs. Kick deletes VODs after about 30 days. Twitch and local files are on the roadmap.
  • Public VODs only. No login, nothing subscriber-only. Not affiliated with Kick.
  • The exe is unsigned. SmartScreen may warn you. Run from source if you'd rather read the code first.
